## A. My Goal — Intention

### What I want to change

I want to take the notch interaction model of apps like Boring Notch and make a more personal interface where I control which widgets appear, how they are arranged, and how they look. I want the notch to feel useful without adding noise to the menu bar or desktop.

### What I want to keep

I want to keep the quick hover-to-expand interaction and the smooth, minimal feel that attracted me to Boring Notch. I also want to use OpenNook's existing window and animation behavior instead of rebuilding it before I understand the framework.

### Why

Widget choice and visual control should make the notch more useful for my own routines while allowing the collapsed state to stay quiet. My initial widget ideas are a music player, a calendar glance, and quick tools. These are intentions, not a description of a finished interface.

### Current result (separate from intention)

I confirmed the app runs, hover expansion/collapse work, and the refined idle notch looks good. I then requested reference-based Calendar, Music, Focus Timer, and Home pages. The latest implementation has a shared fixed viewport, icon navigation, a month/day calendar, Spotify desktop controls and compact live mode, and a dial-style timer. It builds and packages successfully. Date/timer checks and synthetic size checks passed. Spotify Automation approval, actual class/music data, live-notch transitions, and the revised running-app appearance remain **Needs manual verification**. Home content is deferred.
